Bibliography
C. J. Bradley and A. P. Cracknell, The Mathematical Theory of Symmetry in Solids
(Clarendon, Oxford, 1972).
This book is a good source for the method of generating IRs of space groups.
S. C. Miller and W. F. Love, Tables of Irreducible Representations of Space Groups and
Co-Representations of Magnetic Space Groups (Pruett, Boulder, 1967).
This is essentially the same as A. P. Cracknell, B. L. Davies, S. C. Miller, and W. F.
Love, Kronecker Product Tables, Vol. 1 (Plenum, New York, 1979).
These two books contain tables of IRs of space groups.
H. T. Stokes and D. M. Hatch, “Coupled Order Parameters in the Landau Theory of
Phase Transitions in Solids,” Phase Transitions 34, 53–67 (1991).
This paper explains in detail the concepts of primary and secondary order parameters
and how they couple.
D. M. Hatch and H. T. Stokes, “The Complete Listing of Order Parameters for a
Crystalline Phase Transition: A Solution to the Generalized Landau Problem.” Phys.
Rev. B 65, 014113–1–4 (2002).
This paper explains the algorithm for ﬁnding all order parameters, given the change of
symmetry at a phase transition.
H. T. Stokes, D. M. Hatch, and J. D. Wells, “Group-Theoretical Methods for Obtaining
Distortions in Crystals: Applications to Vibrational Modes and Phase Transitions,”
Phys. Rev. B 43, 11010–11018 (1991).
This paper presents an algorithm for generating sets of atomic displacements for a given
IR.
H. T. Stokes and D. M. Hatch, Isotropy Subgroups of the 230 Crystallographic Space
Groups (World Scientiﬁc, Singapore, 1988).
This book contains tables of isotropy subgroups and IR matrices.
stokes.byu.edu/isotropy.html. Internet URL for software packages, isotropy and
isodisplace, which generate information about isotropy subgroup and related atomic
displacements.
