Description: 
ISODISTORT is a user-friendly internet-based tool for exploring the structural distortion modes
of crystalline materials induced by irreducible representations of the parent space-group symmetry.  
The stand-alone ISOVIZ application further allows one to visualize and interactively manipulate the modes generated in ISODISTORT.
